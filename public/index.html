The 4-Week Deep Dive

A structured curriculum designed to shift your mindset from passive hoping to active, intentional desire

WEEK 1

Hope vs. Faith

Understanding the conviction needed to drive real change. Discover the fundamental difference between wishful thinking and faith-driven action that creates tangible results.

WEEK 2

Wishes vs. Desires

Differentiating passive longing from committed, actionable desire. Learn to transform vague aspirations into concrete, measurable goals with clear execution paths.

WEEK 3

Why People Don't Have Desires

Analyzing and removing the barriers that limit ambition and follow-through. Identify and eliminate the mental blocks preventing you from pursuing massive goals.

WEEK 4

Cultivating Desires

Practical strategies for articulating, strengthening, and sustaining massive, impactful goals. Build systems that turn desire into consistent, world-changing action.
